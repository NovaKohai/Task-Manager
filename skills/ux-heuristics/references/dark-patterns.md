# Dark Patterns in UX Design

## 10 Categories

1. **Forced Continuity** - Easy signup, hard cancel
2. **Roach Motel** - Easy in, hard out
3. **Privacy Zuckering** - Tricking into oversharing
4. **Bait and Switch** - Button does something different
5. **Confirmshaming** - Guilt to manipulate opt-in
6. **Hidden Costs** - Fees revealed at final checkout
7. **Misdirection** - Attention drawn away from important info
8. **Trick Questions** - Confusing wording for unintended choices
9. **Sneak into Basket** - Items added without user action
10. **Urgency & Scarcity (False)** - Fake pressure to decide

## Regulatory Context

- **GDPR**: Consent must be freely given, reject as easy as accept, pre-ticked boxes invalid
- **FTC**: Action against hidden fees, difficult cancellation, misleading "free trial"
- **CPRA**: Prohibits dark patterns in opt-out processes

## Audit Checklist & Mirror Test

"Would I feel comfortable if a journalist wrote about how this works?"

## Business Case

- Short-term: higher conversion
- Long-term: lower trust, higher churn, regulatory risk, chargebacks, reputation damage
